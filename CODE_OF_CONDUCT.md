# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MyAroos to be a welcoming space for everyone who contributes, whatever their background, madhab, experience level or nationality. We pledge to act with adab, kindness and honesty in all our interactions.

## Expected behaviour

- Speak with respect and good manners, online as you would in the masjid
- Give and receive feedback gracefully, and critique ideas rather than people
- Assume good intentions and ask before assuming
- Respect differences of scholarly opinion and cite sources when discussing religious content
- Keep conversations on topic and helpful

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, mockery, harassment or personal attacks
- Sectarian hostility or takfir against other contributors
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention of any kind
- Sharing anyone's private information without permission
- Spam, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, open a private report through GitHub or contact the maintainer at https://x.com/PiousGeek. All reports will be handled confidentially.
